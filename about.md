Cody Morterud is a senior software engineer based in Michigan.

He works at Woven by Toyota in Ann Arbor, where he is accountable for release
quality on the Arene Applied Engineering team: he runs verification and
validation campaigns, ensuring quality of product releases.

Before that, he spent five years at Microsoft, on the OneDrive/SharePoint blob
storage persistence layer and AI infrastructure, and two years at Capital One,
owning Java microservices on AWS.

He holds an MS in Computer Science from Georgia Tech (2023) and a BSE in
Computer Science from the University of Michigan (2019).

This site collects his writing on backend systems, distributed computing,
algorithms, performance, and cryptography. His latest side project is
[sim-engine](https://codymorterud.com/sim-engine/), a deterministic real-time
strategy engine he designed and playtested, built with AI coding agents.
